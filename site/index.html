Questions

How do I get the program after paying?

Once PayPal confirms your payment, you are taken to the download. Save the PDF to your phone. Every exercise has a video link inside it.

Do I need a gym?

No. Every session runs on a pitch, a park or any open space. Cones or a few markers help, but shoes or water bottles work too.

How long is each session?

Sessions are short and focused: speed work needs full effort and full rest, not volume. You can run them on days without team training or after a light team session.

What level is it for?

Youth academy players through adult amateurs. If you are under 16, run it with a parent or your coach in the loop and keep the rest periods as written.

I am coming back from an injury. Can I use it?

Only once a physio or doctor has cleared you for full-speed running. This is a performance program, not treatment.
